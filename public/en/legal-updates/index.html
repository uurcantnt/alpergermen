Legislative bulletin.

Amendments published in the Official Gazette that bear on the office's fields of work are collected here in date order. Each entry states the law that introduced the change, the date and issue of the Official Gazette, the date of entry into force and any transitional rule.

Published amendments

The bulletin brings legislative tracking together in one place. The entries below also appear, with their field of law, in the Articles section; the order here follows the date of publication.

Law no. 7589 — Official Gazette, 31 July 2026 (issue 33326)

Deferral of the Announcement of the Judgment: The New Rules

31 August 2026

Paragraphs five to fourteen of article 231 were rewritten: the route of appeal, the court's power to construct a fresh judgment on breach, and the offences excluded from the article.

Related area: Criminal Law · Read the article → Law no. 7589 — Official Gazette, 31 July 2026 (issue 33326)

Hearing Intervals, Remote Hearings and Appeal Limits

31 August 2026

The interval between hearings was capped at three months, the signature rule was relaxed for those attending by video link, and the limits of appeal after the regional court were redrawn.

Related area: Civil Procedure · Read the article → Law no. 7593 — Official Gazette, 18 August 2026 (issue 33344)

Article 31 TPC: The 2026 Change to the Criminal Responsibility of Minors

30 August 2026

Sentence ranges rose in both the 12-15 and 15-18 age bands, and a new paragraph allows the age reduction to be disapplied in cases of intentional killing and injury aggravated by result. It does not apply to acts committed before 18 August 2026.

Related area: Criminal Law · Read the article → Law no. 7589 — Official Gazette, 31 July 2026 (issue 33326)

New Rules for Partition Auctions Under Law No. 7589

30 August 2026

Article 114 of the Enforcement Law changed: for property acquired by inheritance the first auction is held among the co-owning heirs alone, at a bid threshold of the full appraised value. The transitional test is the date of the auction advertisement.

Related area: Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law · Read the article → Law no. 7589 — Official Gazette, 31 July 2026 (issue 33326)

Article 158/4 TPC: Reduced Sentences for Lending a Bank Account

25 August 2026

Where participation in fraud was limited to handing over a bank account, a card or the information needed to use an account, the sentence is halved. The difference between pending and final files and the six-month window are examined.

Related area: Criminal Law · Read the article →

How to read the bulletin

The effect of an amendment on a pending file often depends as much on the transitional rule as on the change itself. For procedural provisions the test is usually the date proceedings were issued; in criminal matters the date of the act; and in enforcement matters most often the date of the auction advertisement.

Each article therefore states the date of entry into force and the transitional rule separately. The bulletin is for general information only and does not constitute legal advice; the effect of an amendment on a particular file is determined by looking at the date of the file together with the stage it has reached.
