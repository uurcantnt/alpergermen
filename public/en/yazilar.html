Criminal Law

Deferral of the Announcement of the Judgment: The New Rules

31 August 2026

Paragraphs five to fourteen of article 231 were rewritten: the route of appeal against a deferral order, the court's power to construct a fresh judgment on breach, and the offences excluded from the article.

Hearing Intervals, Remote Hearings and Appeal Limits

31 August 2026

The interval between hearings was capped at three months, the signature rule was relaxed for those attending by video link, and the limits of appeal after the regional court were redrawn.

Read the article → Criminal Law

Article 31 TPC: The 2026 Change to the Criminal Responsibility of Minors

30 August 2026

Law no. 7593 amended article 31 of the Turkish Penal Code: sentence ranges rose in both the 12-15 and 15-18 age bands, and a new paragraph allows the age reduction to be disapplied in cases of intentional killing and injury aggravated by result. It does not operate retrospectively.

Read the article → Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law

New Rules for Partition Auctions Under Law No. 7589

30 August 2026

Law no. 7589 amended article 114 of the Enforcement Law: for property acquired by inheritance the first auction is held among the co-owning heirs alone, at a bid threshold of the full appraised value. Security exemptions and the fine for unpaid bids are also covered.

Read the article → Criminal Law

Article 158/4 TPC: Reduced Sentences for Lending a Bank Account

25 August 2026

The fourth paragraph added to Article 158 of the Turkish Penal Code by Law No. 7589 halves the sentence where participation in fraud was limited to lending a bank account or card. Scope, the difference between pending and final files, the six-month window and adaptation proceedings are examined in detail.
