Finding the office

The office is on Atatürk Boulevard in the Toros neighbourhood of Konyaaltı, a short distance inland from the Konyaaltı seafront. Coming from central Antalya, the boulevard leads directly to it. For clients flying in, the drive from Antalya Airport takes roughly half an hour depending on traffic, and most seafront hotels in Konyaaltı are within a few minutes by car.

Public transport stops on Atatürk Boulevard are within walking distance of the building. Street parking is available nearby, though spaces can be limited at busier times of day, so allowing a little extra time is sensible. How files are handled in this district is described on the work carried out in the district page.

Ways of meeting

Meetings take three forms. An in-person meeting works best where there are many documents to review together. A telephone call suits short questions about a particular stage of a file, especially where a deadline is close. An online meeting is used by clients living outside Türkiye; when documents are sent in advance, it covers the same ground as a meeting at the office.

Meetings are held in English or Turkish, and no interpreter is needed for English. Where it is not yet clear which area of law a matter falls under, the first meeting is given over to establishing that.

Preparing for an appointment

Sending the documents before the meeting allows a first assessment to be completed in a single session. Contracts, title deeds, payslips, payment receipts, formal warning letters and any envelope in which a court or authority served a document all help. If something has been served on you, the date shown on the envelope matters more than anything else in the file: most periods run from that date.

Documents can be emailed ahead of time, and a translation is not needed at this stage — foreign-language material can be reviewed as it is, with a sworn translation arranged later if the file requires one. If a case or file number already exists, sending it allows the current stage to be checked in advance.

Working hours and urgent matters

Meetings are by appointment. The days shown above indicate when a request can be sent and a file can first be looked at; the hour of the meeting itself is agreed between us. Requests may be sent by telephone, WhatsApp or email, and a reply follows with a suitable time. Clients in other time zones are asked to note their own local time when proposing a slot, so that a workable hour can be found for both sides.

Some situations will not wait. Being taken into custody or called to give a statement, a period running to challenge a detention order, an attachment being levied on assets, or an appeal deadline about to expire all fall into this category. In these cases a direct telephone call allows the position to be assessed without waiting for email.
