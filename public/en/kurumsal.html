When the office was founded, and what it covers

The office opened in Antalya in November 2022. Its founder, Atty. Alper Germen, practises independently as a member of the Antalya Bar Association. Practising independently means the retainer is formed directly between client and lawyer rather than through an institution; in practical terms, responsibility for a file is not divided and the same lawyer carries it from the first meeting to the final decision.

The office works across nine branches of law. What kinds of case fall within each, and how the procedure runs, is set out on the area-by-area detail page. Where a request falls outside that scope, this is said plainly at the first meeting — directing someone to the right forum without losing time matters more than taking the file on.

Bar registration and professional oversight

Practising law in Türkiye requires registration with a bar association. This is not a formality but a continuing supervisory relationship: lawyers registered with the Antalya Bar Association are bound by the Attorneyship Law and by the professional rules of the Union of Turkish Bar Associations. For a client arriving from another legal system, this is often the clearest assurance available.

The framework has concrete consequences. The duty of confidentiality and of care arises from statute and cannot be reduced or waived by agreement. A fee cannot be set below the Minimum Attorney Fee Schedule. And where professional rules are breached, a client may complain to the bar, where the matter is examined by the disciplinary board rather than by the lawyer's own office.

The prohibition on advertising belongs to the same framework. That is why this site carries no case results, client stories, comparative claims or numerical assertions about outcomes: the material here is informational, and no undertaking is given about the outcome of any file.

Following a file and reporting on it

Once a file is opened, the steps ahead and the expected timetable are shared with the client. Hearing dates, interim decisions and submissions made to the court are reported, with an explanation of what each procedural term actually means. The aim is that the client always knows which stage the matter has reached — something that matters more, not less, when the client is in another country.

Reporting is not saved up until after a hearing. When a deadline approaches, when the other side files something, or when anything happens that could change the direction of the file, word goes out without waiting. Where a decision belongs to the client, the options and the likely consequences of each are set out separately; the choice itself remains the client's.

No undertaking is given as to the outcome of proceedings. The strengths and weaknesses of a file are stated openly so that expectations rest on a realistic footing, which is also what prevents unwelcome surprises later in the process.

Who the office works with

The office acts for individuals and for companies. On the individual side, family, criminal, employment, inheritance and property disputes predominate. For companies, the recurring subjects are contract review, recovery of commercial debts and disputes arising out of employment; alongside work on a single file, a continuing advisory relationship can also be arranged.

Given the make-up of Antalya, foreign clients form a substantial part of the practice. In those files, arranging a power of attorney through a consulate, obtaining sworn translations and, where needed, an apostille are all part of the process rather than something the client is left to sort out alone. Meetings are held in Turkish or English, and a file can be run online for clients who remain abroad throughout.

Confidentiality and document security

A lawyer's duty of confidentiality arises under the Attorneyship Law and continues after the retainer has ended. Information a client provides is not used for any purpose beyond the file and is not shared with third parties. A lawyer also has the right to decline to give evidence about what was learned in that capacity.

Documents are taken and retained only so far as conducting the file requires. Personal data is handled within the framework of the Personal Data Protection Law. Material sent digitally is kept in access-restricted storage, and once a file closes, documents are returned or destroyed according to the client's instruction.
