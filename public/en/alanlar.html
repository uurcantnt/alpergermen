The boundaries between these areas are rarely clean. Property that heirs cannot divide raises questions of succession and enforcement at the same time, and how the sale is then conducted is examined under the current rules on partition auctions. In the same way, the outcome of a criminal file can shape a compensation claim arising from the same events.

Rules of procedure cut across every area; the intervals between hearings and the limits on appeal after the regional court are a clear example. On the criminal side, provisions such as the reduction available where involvement was limited to lending an account can change the course of a file outright.
